Returns & refunds

Last updated: September 2026

moravchick is a platform for listing and discovering items — not a party to any sale. Returns and refunds are not handled by moravchick and are solely a matter between the buyer and the seller.

We are a listing platform only

moravchick provides the space to advertise items and to connect buyers with sellers. We do not buy, sell, own, ship or inspect any item, we do not process payments, and we do not offer, fund or manage returns or refunds. Any return, exchange or refund is arranged directly between the buyer and the seller under whatever terms they agree.

Inspect before you pay

Because sales are completed in person, you meet the seller and examine the item before paying — your chance to check condition, size and authenticity. Once you pay and take the item, the transaction is between you and the seller. Most pieces are one-of-a-kind, second-hand items and are generally sold as final sale.

If something goes wrong

  • Raise it directly with the other party in chat — the buyer and seller resolve returns, refunds and disputes between themselves.
  • Each seller sets their own return terms (if any); ask before you buy.
  • moravchick does not mediate, guarantee, refund or reimburse any transaction.
  • You may report a user or listing that breaches our Terms; sellers who repeatedly misrepresent items may be flagged or suspended, but this does not make moravchick responsible for a sale.

Cancellations

Before a meeting, either side may cancel the pending sale in chat, which releases the item back to the feed at no penalty. This only affects the listing on the platform — it is not a refund.
