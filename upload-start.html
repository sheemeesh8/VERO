Photo guide

01Plain backgroundShoot against a clean, even wall or sheet so the item lifts out cleanly for its cut-out cover.
02Fill the frameCentre the piece and get close — leave only a small, even margin around it.
03Soft, even lightNatural daylight near a window is best. Avoid harsh shadows and direct flash.
04Show the detailsAdd close-ups of the fabric/texture, the label or signature, and one angled shot.
05True coloursKeep the colour accurate — don't over-filter. Buyers trust honest photos.
