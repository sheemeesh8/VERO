Pickup & delivery

Last updated: September 2026

moravchick is built around meeting in person, so most sales are completed by local pickup.

Local pickup

After checkout you'll arrange a time and place with the seller in chat. Inspect the item and pay at handover. Sellers set weekly pickup windows you can book from.

Delivery

Some sellers may offer to ship an item. If so, agree the method, cost and timing in chat before you meet or pay. moravchick does not currently handle shipping or tracking on your behalf.

Timing

Items are reserved for 48 hours once a chat opens. If a meeting isn't arranged in that window, the item returns to the public feed.
