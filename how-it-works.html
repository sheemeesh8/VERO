How it works

Last updated: September 2026

moravchick is a curated marketplace for pre-loved fashion and art. Buyers and sellers connect here, agree on a price, and complete the sale in person at a place and time that suits them both.

For buyers

  1. Discover — browse the feed, collections and seasonal sales, or search by item, brand or category.
  2. Save or offer — add pieces to your wishlist, or send the seller a price offer.
  3. Chat — checking out opens a private chat with the seller. You have 48 hours to arrange a meeting.
  4. Meet & pay — inspect the item in person and pay the seller directly at handover.
  5. Confirm — both sides confirm the handover, which updates the item's status and unlocks a mutual rating.

For sellers

  1. List — upload clear photos (see our photo guide), set a price and details.
  2. Set your windows — choose weekly pickup hours buyers can book from.
  3. Negotiate — accept, decline or counter offers in chat.
  4. Hand over — meet the buyer, confirm the sale with a code or QR, and get paid on the spot.
  5. Grow — reliable sellers earn trust badges, higher search priority and buyer confidence.

Meeting a stranger? Please read our Trust & Safety guidance first.
